Fonts embedded in generated certificates
----------------------------------------

SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1 (https://openfontlicense.org). They may be used,
embedded and redistributed, including commercially, provided this notice is kept.
The full Open Sans licence text is in OpenSans-OFL.txt.
The files here are static instances (Regular/Bold weights) generated from the
original variable fonts; the design is unchanged.
